title: "We will continue with our discussion of derivatives of optimal solutions of optimization problems.  We'll discuss the outline of the proof of the theorem introduced at the end of last session, showing that the derivative can typically be expressed as the solution to a constrained quadratic program.  We'll discuss some of the theorem's assumptions, including Robinson's constraint qualification and the extended polyhedricity condition.  We'll also discuss the second-order tangent set that plays a crucial role in the first-order expansion of the optimal solution.  If time permits, we'll also briefly discuss other results about derivatives of optimal solutions in different settings."
